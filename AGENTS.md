# Working on Biblio

Follow the rules in [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md), especially the requirement to keep shared
app features, the demo, and the setup/builder flow in sync. Owner-specific features are local only
when explicitly designated as such. Check for source/deployment divergence before recording or
deploying; the Inbox feature is currently present in the owner's deployed app but absent from this
checkout. Locate and reconcile its newer source before rebuilding that app or its demo.
